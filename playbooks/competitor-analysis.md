# Competitor Analysis Playbook

## Purpose
Understand whether a market offers a real strategic opening rather than merely cataloging competitors.

## Analyze
- offer and price architecture;
- positioning and target persona;
- hero promise and core problem solved;
- product proof and trust mechanisms;
- reviews and recurring complaints;
- landing/PDP structure;
- upsells, bundles and guarantees;
- shipping and returns;
- SEO visibility and content angle;
- ad creative themes when observable;
- obvious GMC/policy weaknesses;
- differentiation that is real vs cosmetic.

## Required conclusions
- what competitors all do similarly;
- what customers appear to value most;
- what customers dislike or distrust;
- where competitors are structurally strong;
- where a new entrant could plausibly differentiate;
- whether the gap is large enough to matter commercially;
- what evidence could invalidate the opportunity.
