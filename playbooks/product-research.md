# Product Research Playbook

## Purpose
Provide a repeatable, strict research procedure that can later be converted into a Hermes skill.

## Inputs
- target market;
- price range;
- margin target;
- product/category exclusions;
- shipping constraints;
- GMC/policy constraints;
- user-specific strategy notes;
- frontier-generated mission brief.

## Procedure
1. Validate hard constraints.
2. Collect a broad candidate pool from approved sources.
3. Remove obvious failures early.
4. Normalize data and deduplicate variants.
5. Score preliminary candidates.
6. Deep-research the strongest subset.
7. Gather competitor, demand, customer, supplier and creative evidence.
8. Record contrary evidence and unknowns.
9. Produce a shortlist with confidence by dimension.
10. Send finalists to adversarial review.
11. Store mission artifacts for later outcome comparison.

## Never do
- fabricate missing evidence;
- treat a viral signal as purchase intent without corroboration;
- hide a weak dimension behind a high total score;
- recommend a product solely because competitors are running ads;
- assume a high retail price implies sufficient margin;
- bypass GMC/policy checks when claims are central to conversion.

## Output
Each finalist must include thesis, evidence, economics, risks, unknowns, score breakdown, contrary case and recommended next validation step.
