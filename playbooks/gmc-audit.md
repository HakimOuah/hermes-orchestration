# GMC Audit Playbook

## Purpose
Reduce Google Merchant Center risk before publication while keeping the audit grounded in current official policy sources.

## Important rule
Google policies change. The agent must not rely solely on remembered rules. Whenever possible, refresh the relevant official Google Merchant Center documentation before a high-stakes audit.

## Audit areas
- product title/description accuracy;
- price and availability consistency;
- shipping information;
- returns/refund policy;
- business identity/contact information;
- misleading or unsupported claims;
- prohibited/restricted product categories;
- medical/health/performance claims when relevant;
- trust and transparency issues;
- consistency between feed, PDP, policies and checkout;
- destination functionality and UX issues likely to trigger disapproval.

## Output
- blockers;
- high-risk issues;
- medium-risk issues;
- low-risk improvements;
- exact evidence/location on the site;
- recommended fix;
- whether a frontier/manual review is required.

This playbook is a pre-check, not a guarantee of Google approval.
