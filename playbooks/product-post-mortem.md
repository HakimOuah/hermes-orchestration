# Product Post-mortem Playbook

## Purpose
Turn each launch into reusable evidence for improving future product selection.

## Inputs
- original research pack;
- original scores and confidence;
- launch decision rationale;
- traffic/ad data;
- conversion metrics;
- AOV, margin and refunds;
- operational issues;
- creative/site changes made after launch.

## Procedure
1. Reconstruct what was believed before launch.
2. Compare predicted vs actual performance.
3. Separate product-market failure from execution failure.
4. Identify over-weighted and under-weighted signals.
5. List missing evidence that would have changed the decision.
6. Compare with previous winners/failures.
7. Propose lessons with evidence strength.
8. Classify each lesson as observation / candidate rule / validated rule / retired rule.
9. Propose scoring or playbook changes if justified.
10. Commit approved learning to Git.

## Key warning
Do not overfit the entire strategy to one winner or one failure.
